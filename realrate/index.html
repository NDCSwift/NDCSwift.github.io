Which side hustle actually pays?

RealRate is a side hustle tracker for iPhone. It takes every expense out of what each hustle earns, divides by the hours you actually worked, and ranks your hustles side by side — so you know which hustle to scale and which to drop.

The problem

You're not measuring what matters.

  • Side income gets judged by the cash that lands, not the hours it took.
  • Gas, platform fees, supplies and the phone plan come out quietly — so the hustle that feels busiest can be the one paying least per hour.
  • Without the number, you can't tell which one it is.

How it works

Log it in 30 seconds. Get one number that tells the truth.

STEP 01

Log the work

One entry holds the money and the hours, because you log after the shift. Prefer to log as you go? Start the live timer and keep it on your Lock Screen.

STEP 02

Add expenses

Platform fees, supplies, gear, the monthly phone plan. Add one-off costs or set recurring ones once. RealRate subtracts what each hustle actually costs you.

STEP 03

See your true rate

Every hustle gets its own number, updated after every session and ranked side by side. Scale the winner. Drop the rest.
